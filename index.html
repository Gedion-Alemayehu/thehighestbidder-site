thehighestbidder

thehighestbidder prepares short-form vertical videos and delivers them straight to a creator's own TikTok inbox as drafts. The creator reviews every draft and decides what gets published.

What it does

Creators who post on a consistent schedule spend most of their time on assembly rather than ideas: writing a hook, recording narration, cutting clips to the narration, timing captions, and normalising audio so the result doesn't sound quiet next to everything else in the feed. thehighestbidder automates that assembly step.

  • Drafts a hook and a beat-by-beat script for a chosen format.
  • Generates narration and cuts each shot to the length of its own narration line, so nothing is clipped.
  • Times on-screen captions to the spoken audio and keeps them clear of TikTok's interface.
  • Normalises loudness to broadcast level and exports vertical 1080×1920.
  • Delivers the finished file to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft.

Nothing publishes itself

thehighestbidder uploads drafts only. Every video is finished and published by the creator inside the TikTok app, where they add sound, adjust the caption, and apply TikTok's AI-generated content label before posting.

How it uses TikTok

A creator connects their own TikTok account using TikTok's official login. thehighestbidder requests the minimum permissions it needs:

PermissionWhy it is requested
user.info.basicTo confirm which account is connected and show it back to the creator.
video.uploadTo deliver a prepared video to that creator's own TikTok inbox as a draft.

thehighestbidder does not read a creator's videos, followers, messages, or analytics, and it cannot publish on their behalf.

Generated content and disclosure

Video and narration produced by thehighestbidder are generated using AI tools. Creators are prompted to apply TikTok's AI-generated content label before publishing, and thehighestbidder sets the AI-generated content flag on any upload that supports it.
