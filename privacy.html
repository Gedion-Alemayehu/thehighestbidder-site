Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

This policy explains what information thehighestbidder ("we", "the service") accesses when you connect your TikTok account, why we access it, where it is kept, and how to remove it. It applies to the thehighestbidder software and to this website.

1. Information we collect

TikTok account information

When you connect your TikTok account through TikTok's official login, TikTok provides us with:

  • An account identifier (open_id) and basic profile information such as your display name and avatar, under the user.info.basic permission. We use this only to confirm which account is connected and display it back to you.
  • An access token and refresh token, which authorise the service to deliver video drafts to your TikTok inbox under the video.upload permission.

Content you create

Video files, narration audio, captions, and the text prompts used to produce them. These are created by you through the service and stored on the computer where you run it.

What we do not collect

  • Your TikTok password. Authentication happens entirely on TikTok's own login page; we never see it.
  • Your existing TikTok videos, drafts, followers, direct messages, comments, or analytics. The permissions we request do not grant access to any of these.
  • Any information about other TikTok users.
  • Advertising identifiers, browsing history, or location data. We run no analytics or advertising trackers on this website.

2. How we use information

We use the information above for one purpose: to deliver video drafts you have created to your own TikTok inbox, and to show you which account those drafts will go to. We do not sell, rent, or share this information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Where information is stored

thehighestbidder runs on your own computer. TikTok tokens are stored in a configuration file in your user account's home directory with file permissions restricting access to your user only. Video files are stored in the working directory you choose. We do not operate a server that stores your TikTok tokens or your videos.

4. Third parties

thehighestbidder can call third-party AI services to generate script text, narration audio, and video clips. Only your own prompts and scripts are sent to those services. No TikTok account information, tokens, or profile data is ever transmitted to them. Where such a service is used, its own privacy policy governs the data you send it.

The only party that receives your TikTok account information is TikTok itself, when the service calls TikTok's API on your behalf.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only for as long as your account is connected. You can remove everything at any time:

  • Disconnect from TikTok's side: in the TikTok app, go to Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions, and remove thehighestbidder. This immediately invalidates the tokens we hold.
  • Delete locally stored data: delete the configuration directory the service created in your home folder, along with any videos in your working directory.
  • Request assistance: email us at the address below and we will help you confirm removal.

TikTok access tokens expire 24 hours after issuance and refresh tokens expire 365 days after issuance. Expired tokens are replaced or become unusable automatically.

6. Your rights

Because the data lives on your own machine and is limited to what is listed above, you can access, correct, export, or delete it directly at any time. If you are in a jurisdiction granting statutory rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, or objection, you may also exercise those rights by contacting us; we will respond within 30 days.

7. Children

thehighestbidder is not directed to children. You must be at least 18 years old, or the age of majority where you live, to use it. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Security

We restrict token files to your user account and transmit all data to TikTok over HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure; if you believe your tokens have been exposed, revoke the app's permission in TikTok immediately using the steps in section 5.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be described on this page.

10. Contact

Email projectgedion@gmail.com with any question about this policy or any request concerning your data.
